28-DAY PROGRESSION

Build your level week by week.

WEEK 1 — FOUNDATION

Learn the movements. Prioritise clean technique and control.

WEEK 2 — CONSISTENCY

Repeat movements with better balance and reliable execution.

WEEK 3 — CONTROLLED SPEED

Increase execution speed while maintaining technical quality.

WEEK 4 — CONSOLIDATION

Bring skills together and assess your progress.

BEFORE EVERY SESSION

Warm-up & recovery

General movement — 3–5 min
Easy jogging, brisk movement or skipping.

Dynamic mobility — 4–5 min
Prepare hips, ankles, knees and upper body.

Movement preparation — 3–5 min
Light movement and easy ball touches.

Cooldown — 5 min
Walk easily and allow your body to recover.
